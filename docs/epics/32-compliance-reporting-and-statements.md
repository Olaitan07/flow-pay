# Epic 32 — Compliance, Reporting & Statements

## Objective

Provide financial records required by customers, operations teams, finance teams, auditors, and applicable regulatory processes.  

## User Story — Customer Statement

As a customer,  
I want to view my transaction statement,  
so that I can understand activity on my account.  

## Functional Requirements

Statements should support:  
Defined date range.  
Transaction reference.  
Transaction date.  
Transaction type.  
Amount.  
Currency.  
Status.  
Fees where applicable.  
Operational reports may include:  
Successful transactions.  
Failed transactions.  
Pending transactions.  
Reversals.  
Fees.  
Settlement records.  
Reconciliation mismatches.  
Access to reports must respect authorization and data privacy.  

## Acceptance Criteria

A customer statement must contain only financial information the customer is authorized to access.  
Historical reporting must reflect recorded financial history rather than silently rewriting previous transactions.
